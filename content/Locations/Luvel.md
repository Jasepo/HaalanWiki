* **Overview:** A vast coastal-desert region famous for commerce and its control of Balchur Pass, the great water route through the central mountains. Luvel is industrious, practical, and politically neutral.

* **Starting Location:** The adventure begins here in Luvel.

* **Key Districts:**
  
  * **The Dustward:** Originally the portion of Luvel facing the open desert; poorer construction and blowing sand made it undesirable. Now synonymous with the city's poor quarter (the working-class neighborhoods, workshops, and modest businesses).
  
  * **The Shallows:** The poorest district in Luvel near low-lying waterfront areas, historically prone to flooding and sewage problems. You can find the Broken Shillelagh here, operated by [Caldeer](..\NPCs\Caldeer.md), and that's where [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) lives.
  
  * **The Crossways:** The market and commercial district featuring the massive Sunmarket shopping center in the middle. Most merchants live here. [Belinda Potts](..\NPCs\Belinda%20Potts.md) lives here. And [The Brasswright Guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md) is also there, where [Professor Marchenko](..\NPCs\Professor%20Marchenko.md) resides.
  
  * **The Heights**: where wealthy residents and influential families live.
  
  * **The Stonewall**: the ordinary residential neighborhoods
  
  * **The Light Ward**: which is the government offices, ports, civic institutions, and educational facilities. "The House of Open Pages" is in the Light Ward district

**[Dread Barrens](Dread%20Barrens.md):** Scorching desert near Luvel featuring old ruins, battlefields, giant bugs, jackals, and surface-roaming Bulettes.

**Balchur Pass:** Major geographic mountain water route controlled by Luvel.

**Curlew Dunes Memory Gardens:** The cemetery grounds have fragrant desert roses (the regional flower of [Regalla](Regalla.md)). Due to the sudden spike in [Red Lung](..\Red%20Lung.md) fatalities over the past two weeks, groundskeepers are actively working to expand the perimeter with newly dug plots.

**The Sunmarket:**

* **The Rolling Pin:** Renowned bakery operated by halfling baker Fenwick Thistledown and his lookalike kin; famous for artisan breads, cornbread, and fresh pastries.

* **The Saffron Serpent:** A spice and herb pavilion managed by Madame Saffira, carrying exotic herbs, nightshade, Canis root, moon sugar, and lavender.

* **The Black Kettle:** A popular coffeehouse and community hub established by Garrick Blackbrew; specializes in triple-shot espressos, cold brews, and imported teas.

* **Bramblewick’s Basket:** An outdoor produce stall run by the elderly forest gnome Nessa Bramblewick; stocks fresh regional produce and snowberries.

* **Butterbarrel’s Best:** A dairy and livestock stall managed by a hill dwarf, Dorrin; sells goat milk, assorted artisan cheeses, and features "award-winning" show goats (e.g., Malek and Eddie).

* ***Just Mentioned:*** The Gilded Quill (bookstore/cartography shop run by Eldrin Vale), Butcher’s Block (meats run by Grand Amaro), and Root & Remedy (herbalist run by Eliswyn Mosswhipper).

#### Economy & Exports

* **Major Exports:** Alcohol (rum, tequila, vodka), weapons, spices (dried peppers, cumin, coriander), ceramics, and pottery. Trade is primarily conducted with [Izedel](Izedel.md) and [Regalla](Regalla.md) via established seafaring routes.
* **Political Stance:** Politically neutral, maintaining that [Haalan](Haalan.md) is most prosperous when trade and regional stability are preserved.
* **Geographic Control:** Controls the **Balchur Pass**, a critical mountain water route opening, serving as a primary transit lane across the continental split.
