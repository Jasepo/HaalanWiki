- **Overview:** A vast coastal-desert region famous for commerce and its control of Balchur Pass, the great water route through the central mountains. Luvel is industrious, practical, and politically neutral.

- **Starting Location:** The adventure begins here in Luvel.

- **Key Districts:**

    - **The Dustward:** Originally the portion of Luvel facing the open desert; poorer construction and blowing sand made it undesirable. Now synonymous with the city's poor quarter (the working-class neighborhoods, workshops, and modest businesses).
    
    - **The Shallows:** The poorest district in Luvel near low-lying waterfront areas, historically prone to flooding and sewage problems. You can find the Broken Shillelagh here operated by [[NPCs/Caldeer|Caldeer]], and that's where [[PCs/Thresher|Thresher]] lives.
    
    - **The Crossways:** The market and commercial district featuring the massive Sunmarket shopping center in the middle. Most merchants live here. [[NPCs/Belinda Potts|Belinda Potts]] lives here. And [[The Brasswright Guild|The Brasswright Guild]] where [[NPCs/Professor Marchenko|Professor Marchenko]] resides.
    
    - **The Heights**: where wealthy residents and influential families live.
    
    - **The Stonewall**: the ordinary residential neighborhoods
    
    - **The Light Ward**: which is the government offices, ports, civic institutions, and educational facilities. "The House of Open Pages" is in the Light Ward district

- **Notable Landmarks:** Balchur Pass, Curlew Dunes Memory Gardens (where [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]]'s funeral is held).

**Dread Barrens:** Scorching desert near Luvel featuring old ruins, battlefields, giant bugs, jackals, and surface-roaming Bulettes.

**Balchur Pass:** Major geographic mountain water route controlled by Luvel.

#### Economy & Exports

- **Major Exports:** Alcohol (rum, tequila, vodka), weapons, spices (dried peppers, cumin, coriander), ceramics, and pottery. Trade is primarily conducted with [[Izedel]] and [[Regalla]] via established seafaring routes.
- **Political Stance:** Politically neutral, maintaining that [[Haalan]] is most prosperous when trade and regional stability are preserved.
- **Geographic Control:** Controls the **Balchur Pass**, a critical mountain water route opening, serving as a primary transit lane across the continental split.
