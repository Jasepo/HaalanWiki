* **Overview:** A vast coastal-desert region famous for commerce and its control of Balchur Pass, the great water route through the central mountains. Luvel is industrious, practical, and politically neutral.

* **Starting Location:** The adventure begins here in Luvel.

* **Key Districts:**
  
  * **The Dustward:** Originally the portion of Luvel facing the open desert; poorer construction and blowing sand made it undesirable. Now synonymous with the city's poor quarter.
  
  * **The Shallows:** The poorest district in Luvel near low-lying waterfront areas, historically prone to flooding and sewage problems.
  
  * **The Crossways:** The market and commercial district featuring the massive Sunmarket shopping center in the middle. Most merchants live here. Belinda Potts lives here.

* **Notable Landmarks:** Balchur Pass, Curlew Dunes Memory Gardens (where Valdemar's funeral is held).

**Dread Barrens:** Scorching desert near Luvel featuring old ruins, battlefields, giant bugs, jackals, and surface-roaming Bulettes.

**Balchur Basin/Pass:** Major geographic mountain water route controlled by Luvel.
