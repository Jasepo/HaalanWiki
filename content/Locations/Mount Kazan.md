Harsh snow-capped mountain range in the south. Mount Kazan is close to [Izedel](Izedel.md), but even closer to *Frostfall*.

A major entrance to the [Umberdeep](..\The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md) is located inside Mount Kazan, with additional rumored openings in the harsh desert of the [Barrens](..\Dread%20Barrens.md) north of [Luvel](Luvel.md).
