Harsh snow-capped mountain range in the south. Mount Kazan is close to [[Izedel]], but even closer to [[Izedel|Frostfall]].

A major entrance to the [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umberdeep]] is located inside Mount Kazan, with additional rumored openings in the harsh desert of the [[Dread Barrens|Barrens]] north of [[Luvel]].
