**Overview:** A northern power with a strong regional identity and forest [gnomes origin](..\Character%20Ancestries%20&%20Origins.md). Its leaders are widely believed to have ambitions beyond their current position within the union and watch [Borgan](Borgan.md) closely.

* **Culture & Symbolism:** Defined by dense northern forests and wilderness. Its cultural identity centers around a wolf head symbol representing intimidation.
* **Political Stance:** Acts as a primary challenger to [Borgan](Borgan.md)'s crown alongside [Izedel](Izedel.md).
