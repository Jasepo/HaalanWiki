* **Overview:** A cosmopolitan eastern coastal power with strong maritime traditions, rock gnomes origin, and a large immigrant population. Its position on the Aerrton Sea made it a natural entry point for travelers and foreign cultures. Izedel is one of the regions rumored to be reconsidering Borgan's dominance.

* **History:** Once an independent nation and one of the six powers in the Thousand Years of War. Its historic capital is also called Izedel (a massive coastal city with ~1.2 million people).

* **Main Settlements:**
  
  * **Izedel** (Former capital / major city)
  
  * **Shemack** (City)
  
  * **Cayholm** (City)
  
  * **Frostfall** (Town)
  
  * **Sutland** (Town)

* **Culture & Symbols:** Outward-looking toward the water. Its ancient coat of arms depicts three fish (predating Haalan, symbolizing marine cultural background). Historically the center of worship of Haalan's Moon God (Selvara), associated with tides, navigation, sailors, and fishermen.
