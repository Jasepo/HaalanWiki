Wooded area with a strange, maddening silence and magical atmosphere, home to a hag.

**[[Borgan]]** and **Silent Hollow** are both located on the western half of the [[Haalan|continent]], with [[Borgan]] sitting further north and Silent Hollow positioned in the southwestern forested region.

Measuring the straight-line distance between them, they are approximately **120 to 150 kilometers** apart. (Non-canonical estimation)

#### Hazards & Lore

- **Supernatural Silence:** A heavily avoided wooded area characterized by a strange, unnatural quiet that many believe directly causes visitors to lose their minds.
- **Monstrous Rumors:** Widely rumored to be the domain of a hag or other unknown monstrosities.
- **Survivor Status:** No one has ever returned with a coherent account; those who make it back are entirely deranged and babbling, while the majority never return at all.
