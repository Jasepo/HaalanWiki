Haalan is a nation born from a thousand years of war. For thousands of years, six societies divided by mountains, rivers, deserts, and seas fought continuously while the [[Gods, Faith, & The Forsaken|gods]] watched. Armies marched across [[Locations/Borgan|Borgan]] beneath gold and crimson banners, warships burned along the shores of [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]], and the deserts of [[Locations/Luvel|Luvel]] swallowed the bones of soldiers. Forests were felled, cities were burned, and generations knew nothing but war until the kingdoms grew tired of burying their own children, bringing six crowns together so six nations became one to form a new kingdom: Haalan. Its size and scale are modeled after real-world Australia, featuring six diverse geographic terrains: hot deserts in the east ([[Locations/Luvel|Luvel]]), mountain ranges in the south ([[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]]), grasslands in the northeast ([[Locations/Mivolan|Mivolan]]), forests west of Balchur Pass ([[Locations/Borgan|Borgan]]), swamp lands in the far west ([[Locations/Drabasha|Drabasha]]), and tropical coastal islands in the north ([[Locations/Regalla|Regalla]]).

- **Current Date & Time:** The campaign begins on **[[Calendar, Time, & Holidays|Sulday, the 7th of Vigus 275 A.W]].** (After War), just six days after First Morning, at the beginning of a new year.

- **Eras & History:** The modern Unity began 275 years ago, marked as A.W. (After War). The exact length of the Thousand Years' War remains unknown as records were burned and rewritten over time.

- **Continental Geography:** A massive ancient earthquake split the eastern and western halves of the continent down the middle of the mountains, creating a major central waterway controlled by [[Luvel]].

- **The World Right Now:**

    - **The King is Dead:** King Boleslaus II recently passed away, and his young successor, King Lambert Boleslaus, has taken the throne. Rumors are spreading, tainting the royal image.
    
    - **The Red Lung:** A natural, non-magical, contagious respiratory illness spreading across [[Locations/Haalan|Haalan]] for approximately 9 months. Severe cases cause intense physical and breathing strain, leaving victims weak, breathless, coughing blood, or dead. Because healing resources, clerics, and priests are scarce and rapidly overwhelmed, fatalities are high. This crisis has driven significant growth in the forbidden worship of [[Gods, Faith, & The Forsaken|Velshara]], the [[Gods, Faith, & The Forsaken|Forsaken]] Goddess of decay.
    
    - **Uneasy Peace:** [[Locations/Mivolan|Mivolan]] and [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]] watch [[Locations/Borgan|Borgan]] closely, [[Locations/Regalla|Regalla]] questions its place in the union, [[Locations/Luvel|Luvel]] remains neutral, and [[Locations/Drabasha|Drabasha]] supports the Crown. Many fear civil war is brewing.
    
    - **Trade and Travel:** Roads remain mostly open, but quarantines, disrupted commerce, bandits, and monsters make long journeys uncertain. (e.g., [[Locations/Luvel|Luvel]] to [[Locations/Borgan|Borgan]] takes about 32 days of travel).
