Haalan, a nation born from a thousand years of war. Its size and scale are modeled after real-world Australia, featuring six diverse geographic terrains: hot deserts in the east ([Luvel](Luvel.md)), mountain ranges in the south ([Izedel](Izedel.md)), grasslands in the northeast ([Mivolan](Mivolan.md)), forests west of Balchur Basin ([Borgan](Borgan.md)), swamp lands in the far west ([Drabasha](Drabasha.md)), and tropical coastal islands in the north ([Regalla](Regalla.md)).

* **Current Date & Time:** The campaign begins on **Sulday, the 7th of Vigus 275 A.W.** (After War), just six days after First Morning, at the beginning of a new year.

* **The World Right Now:**
  
  * **The King is Dead:** King Boleslaus II recently passed away, and his young successor, King Lambert Boleslaus, has taken the throne. Rumors are spreading, tainting the royal image.
  
  * **The Red Lung:** A contagious respiratory illness is spreading across Haalan. Severe cases leave victims weak, breathless, coughing blood, or dead. Healers are scarce, and worship of the Forsaken Goddess of decay, Velshara, is growing around this crisis.
  
  * **Uneasy Peace:** Mivolan and Izedel watch Borgan closely, Regalla questions its place in the union, Luvel remains neutral, and Drabasha supports the Crown. Many fear civil war is brewing.
  
  * **Trade and Travel:** Roads remain mostly open, but quarantines, disrupted commerce, bandits, and monsters make long journeys uncertain. (e.g., Luvel to Borgan takes about 32 days of travel).
