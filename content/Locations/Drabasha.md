**Overview:** A traditional supporter of [Borgan](Borgan.md) situated in swamplands far to the west. It is the region most firmly aligned with the Crown.

* **Geography & Economy:** A poorer, swampy region influenced by runoff from Haalan Bay. Due to economic hardship, it remains firmly loyal to [Borgan](Borgan.md) and relies heavily on the capital for financial and military support.
