**Overview:** A west maritime region on the Haalan Bay featuring tropical islands in the north. An increasing number of Regallans openly question whether Regalla should remain part of [Haalan](Haalan.md) at all.

* **Political Stance:** Comprises northern islands seeking complete secession from [Haalan](Haalan.md) to form an independent nation, rather than contesting [Borgan](Borgan.md) for leadership of the Unity.
