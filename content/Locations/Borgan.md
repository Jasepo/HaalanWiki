* **Overview:** The political heart of [Haalan](Haalan.md) and the seat of the Crown. It is the nation's largest city and remains the strongest center of government, wealth, and institutional power. Borgan suffered less during the long wars and emerged as the strongest city.

* **Magic & Culture:** Home to the most prestigious university for higher learning in all schools of magic (though expensive). Borgan is traditional, looking toward the interior of [Haalan](Haalan.md) and its institutional authority.

* **Institutions:** Home of the **[Grand Arcanum](..\Grand%20Arcanum.md)**, a massive university of higher magical learning.

* **Political Status:** Acts as the seat of the Crown and political center of the Unity. Following the death of the previous king and ascension of his young successor, Borgan faces growing political tension and ambition from regional powers.

* **Military:** Royal Crown Guards wear distinct **crimson and gold** armor. The new monarch, King Lambert Boleslaus, took the throne at age 15 in a suspiciously rushed coronation; many view him as a puppet, while factions within the court operate through covert channels.
