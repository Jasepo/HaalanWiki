**Overview:** A rugged settlement located deep within the mountain ranges, serving as the headquarters for the [[Black Lanterns|Black Lanterns]]. It is a practical, isolated town focused on defense and survival.

- **The Black Lanterns:** Home base for the faction. Most of their work involves holding back monsters emerging from the [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umberdeep]] in the surrounding mountains. Because of this, the Lanterns rarely venture to places like [[Borgan]] or [[Drabasha]]; there is simply no work for them there.
- **Reputation:** To outsiders, the settlement and its people are often viewed as a dark, shady, and mysterious organization. 
- **[[The Brasswright Guild]]:** Hosts one of the most prominent chapters of the [[The Brasswright Guild|guild]], likely providing the necessary tools, weapons, and engineering support to hold the mountain line.
