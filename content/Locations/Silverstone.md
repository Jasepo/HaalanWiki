**Overview:** A rugged settlement located deep within the mountain ranges, serving as the headquarters for the [Black Lanterns](..\Black%20Lanterns.md). It is a practical, isolated town focused on defense and survival.

* **The Black Lanterns:** Home base for the faction. Most of their work involves holding back monsters emerging from the [Umberdeep](..\The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md) in the surrounding mountains. Because of this, the Lanterns rarely venture to places like [Borgan](Borgan.md) or [Drabasha](Drabasha.md), there is simply no work for them there.
* **Reputation:** To outsiders, the settlement and its people are often viewed as a dark, shady, and mysterious organization.
* **The Brasswright Guild:** Hosts one of the most prominent chapters of the [Brasswright Guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md), likely providing the necessary tools, weapons, and engineering support to hold the mountain line.
