* **Overview:** The physical underworld beneath the surface. It is wet, dark, and dangerous. There is no royalty overlooking it, so people fend for themselves, living in small settlements closer to the surface for basic necessities.

* **Inhabitants:** Drow, Duergar, and Svirfneblin (Umberdeep Elves, Dwarves, and Gnomes). Deep gnomes originate here and in [Mount Kazan](Locations\Mount%20Kazan.md).

* **Culture:** Folks are used to dangerous lives. The [Umberdark](The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md) language is commonly spoken among underground communities. Monster hunter guilds thrive here.
