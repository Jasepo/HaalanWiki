* **Overview:** The physical underworld beneath the surface. It is wet, dark, and dangerous. There is no royalty overlooking it, so people fend for themselves, living in small settlements closer to the surface for basic necessities.

* **Inhabitants:** Drow, Duergar, and Svirfneblin (Umberdeep Elves, Dwarves, and Gnomes). Deep gnomes originate here and in [Mount Kazan](Locations\Mount%20Kazan.md). Beyond monsters, the Umberdeep features hardened, self-reliant local communities and skilled subterranean warriors who know how to survive the harsh dark.

* **Culture:** Folks are used to dangerous lives. The [Umberdark](The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md) language is commonly spoken among underground communities. Monster hunter guilds thrive here.

* **Black Mushroom Tea:** A potent, tar-black subterranean tea brewed from fungi native to the [Umberdeep](The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md). While possessing negligible hallucinogenic properties when properly prepared, it is extraordinarily high in caffeine, emitting an earthy, mushroom-like aroma with sweet undertones of wild cherries. Favored by subterranean dwellers and heavy stimulant users.

#### Entrances & Accessibility

* **Physical Connection:** Accessible from the surface by digging deep down.
* **Entrances:** Most major entrances are known and protected to prevent surface monster incursions, though new openings emerge over time.
* **Notable Locations:** A major entrance is located inside [Mount Kazan](Locations\Mount%20Kazan.md), with additional rumored openings in the harsh desert of the [Barrens](Locations\Dread%20Barrens.md) north of [Luvel](Locations\Luvel.md).
