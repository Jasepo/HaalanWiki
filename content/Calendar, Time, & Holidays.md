A [Haalani](Locations\Haalan.md) year contains 364 days, divided into 13 months of 28 days each. Every month therefore consists of exactly four seven-day weeks, giving the year exactly 52 weeks. The dates always fall on the same weekday, making a [Haalani](Locations\Haalan.md) calendar easy to understand.

The [campaign](Campaign%20Dashboard.md) begins on **Sulday, the 7th of Vigus 275 A.W.**, just six days after First Morning, at the beginning of a new year.

## Days of the Week

* **Varday** always falls on a 1st, 8th, 15th, and 22nd.
* **Thaelday** always falls on 2nd, 9th, 16th, and 23rd.
* **Wisdary** always falls on 3rd, 10th, 17th, and 24th.
* **Selday** always falls on 4th, 11th, 18th, and 25th.
* **Ravday** always falls on 5th, 12th, 19th, and 26th.
* **Tolday** always falls on 6th, 13th, 20th, and 27th.
* **Sulday** always falls on 7th, 14th, 21st, and 28th.

## Holidays (not a complete list)

* **Vigus 1 – First Morning:** [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md)'s New Year Celebration, associated with a fresh start.
* **Demus 28 – Haali:** Celebration of Pride, associated with rights, visibility, and acceptance of everyone.
* **Bitus 12 – Founder's Feast:** [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md)-wide celebration emphasizing that no one should eat alone.
* **Progus 18 – [Luveli](Locations\Luvel.md) celebration:** Rooted in worship of [Solara](Gods,%20Faith,%20&%20The%20Forsaken.md).
* **Addus 1 – Day of the Dead:** Celebration of those that have passed to the Final Shore.

## Eras & Seasons

* **A.W. (After War):** The modern era of [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md).
* **B.W. (Before the War):** Often ancient history that has been written, rewritten, and destroyed all over again.
* **T.Y.W. (Thousand Year War):** The details of that history can be muddy due to "history being written by victor".

Seasons go in a cycle of Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter... with each season lasting a little over three months. Some seasons tend to drag, especially an extra hot Summer or an unusually cold Winter.
