- **Calendar System:** A [[Locations/Haalan|Haalani]] year contains 364 days, divided into 13 months of 28 days each (52 weeks exactly). Dates always fall on the same weekday.

- **Days of the Week:** Varday ($1, 8, 15, 22$), Thaelday ($2, 9, 16, 23$), Wisdary ($3, 10, 17, 24$), Selday ($4, 11, 18, 25$), Ravday ($5, 12, 19, 26$), Tolday ($6, 13, 20, 27$), Sulday ($7, 14, 21, 28$).

- **Months (13):** Vigus, Radus, Demus, Bitus, Progus, Lorus, Tempus, Turmus, Cerpus, Accus, Addus, Vulpus, Galus.

- **Holidays:**

    - **Vigus 1:** First Morning (New Year Celebration, fresh start).
    
    - **Demus 28:** Haali (Celebration of Pride, rights, visibility, and acceptance).
    
    - **Bitus 12:** Founder's Feast (No one should eat alone).
    
    - **Progus 18:** Luveli celebration rooted in worship of Solara.
    
    - **Addus 1:** Day of the Dead (Celebration of those passed to the Final Shore).
    
- **Eras:** A.W. (After War - modern era), B.W. (Before the War), T.Y.W. (Thousand Year War).
## Seasons & Weather Cycles

- **Cycle:** Seasons follow a pattern of Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter, Spring... with each season lasting a little over three months.

- **Variations:** Some seasons tend to drag, particularly an unusually hot summer or a bitter winter.
