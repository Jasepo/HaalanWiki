## The Haalani Calendar System

A [Haalani](Locations\Haalan.md) year contains **364 days**, divided into **13 months of 28 days each** (52 weeks exactly). Because 28 divides evenly by 7, **dates always fall on the same weekday** every year.

### Holidays

* **Vigus 1 — First Morning:** New Year celebration; a fresh start.
* **Demus 28 — Haali:** Celebration of pride, rights, visibility, and acceptance.
* **Bitus 12 — Founder's Feast:** No one should eat alone.
* **Progus 18 — Luveli Celebration:** A festival rooted in worship of Solara.
* **Addus 1 — Day of the Dead:** Celebration of those passed to the Final Shore.

### Eras

* **A.W. — After War:** The modern era (current year: 275 A.W.).
* **B.W. — Before the War.**
* **T.Y.W. — Thousand Year War.**

## Seasons & Weather

* **Cycle:** Winter → Spring → Summer → Fall → Winter → Spring...
* **Length:** Each season lasts a little over three months.
* **Variations:** Some seasons drag — an unusually hot summer, or a bitter winter.
