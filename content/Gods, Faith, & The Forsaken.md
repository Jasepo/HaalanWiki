* **The Origin of Faith:** According to ancient traditions, Gods once lived physically in the Mortal World. Mortals eventually rejected the Gods' commands to fight their divine wars, made peace, and built civilizations. Those who chose worship were cast into the underground ([Umberdeep](The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md)).

* **Clerics in Haalan:** Clerics do not need to worship a god. Divine magic comes from absolute conviction in a belief, teaching, community, philosophy, or spiritual tradition (faith itself). Congregations are communities built around shared teachings rather than traditional temples.

* **The Original Six Gods:**
  
  * **\[Varkos\]:** God of Death and War (associated with [Borgan](Locations\Borgan.md)). Opposite of Solara.
  
  * **\[Solara\]:** Sun Goddess, associated with [Luvel](Locations\Luvel.md), Light, and preservation of Life. Opposite of Varkos.
  
  * **\[Selvara\]:** Moon Goddess, associated with [Izedel](Locations\Izedel.md), the sea, navigation, tides, and sailors. Opposite of Ravel.
  
  * **\[Ravel\]:** Trickster God, associated with [Mivolan](Locations\Mivolan.md), riddles, puzzles, festivals, and masks. Opposite of Selvara.
  
  * **\[Odrun\]:** God of Knowledge, associated with [Drabasha](Locations\Drabasha.md), books, scrolls, and research. Opposite of Thaelwyn.
  
  * **\[Thaelwyn\]:** God of Nature, associated with the wild, untamed ocean and Regalla islands. Opposite of Odrun.

* **The Three Forsaken (Forbidden Cults):**
  
  * \[Azrathul\] (The King Beneath): Believes freedom is the source of mortal suffering. One ruler, one law, one purpose.
  
  * \[Velshara\] (The Mother of Worms):  Associated with decay. Teaches that rot feeds growth and disease is part of the natural cycle. Growing in relevance during the [Red Lung](Red%20Lung.md).
  
  * \[Nhal\] (The Empty God): Associated with forbidden knowledge, prophecy, dreams, and cosmic truths. Some scholars doubt Nhal ever existed.
