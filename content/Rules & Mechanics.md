
**Starting Level & Stats:** Level 1 start with Point Buy for stats.

**Firearms:** Exist, but gunpowder is scarce in the wilderness and must be purchased or made. Crossbolts and arrows are more easily available.

**Languages:** Common, Dwarvish, Elvish, Halfling, Gnomish, Umberdark ([[The Umberdeep & Underground|underground]]), Sylvan ([[The Six Realms|Everweald]]).

**Magic Items & Potions:** Most magic items are found rather than bought due to high cost in metropolitan areas. Artificers can craft items, but finding wealthy buyers can be tough. Potions can be brewed if you have recipes, ingredients, and a workspace. Scrolls are rare but can be used by anyone.

**Sample Duration:** Traveling from [[Luvel]] to [[Borgan]] takes about 32 days of travel, making extensive journeys something that must be carefully calculated.

#### Resting & Inspiration

- **Resting Rules:** Characters can take up to two short rests (at least 1 hour each) before a long rest (at least 8 hours) is required.
- **Inspiration:** Following 5.5e rules, humans gain 1 inspiration point after every long rest. Inspiration can also be earned through creative problem solving.
- **Leveling & Combat:** Starting at level 1 using traditional XP progression. Levels 1–3 are exceptionally lethal.
