
**Overview:** Belinda Potts is a pragmatic dwarven merchant based in the coastal city of [[Luvel]]. She was married to the adventurer [[Valdemar Potts]], whom she once handed a strict ultimatum: settle down and make a home with her, or keep wandering the road alone. Choosing Belinda, [[Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] hung up his sword, and together the couple opened a shop to sell off the vast hoard of gear, artifacts, and strange relics he had accumulated across his years of adventuring.

Though [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] has since passed away, Belinda continues to run the shop on her own. Because his collection was so immense, even after all these years she still hasn't managed to sell through it all, making her store an essential stop for travelers looking for rare, unusual, and one-of-a-kind oddities.

Has a twin brother called [[Byron]]


## Message to Corben about Valdemar's death

Dear [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]], 

I wish I were writing to you under happier circumstances. 

My husband, [[Valdemar Potts]], passed away from the sickness three days ago. We knew he was ill, and we knew what that could mean, but I don't think either of us was prepared for how quickly the end would come. In his final days, [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] spoke often about his life and the people who had been part of it. He spoke of [[Corben Wigglehand|you]]. Whatever roads may have taken you apart since you last saw one another, I want you to know that he remembered you fondly. 

We will be laying [[Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] to rest here in [[Luvel]]. I know travel has become difficult, and perhaps even dangerous, so I understand if you cannot come. But if circumstances allow it, I would be grateful to have you here.

I think it would have meant the world to [[Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] to know that the people who mattered to him were together one last time on his behalf. 

The funeral will be held on [[Calendar, Time, & Holidays|Sulday the 7th of Vigus]] at Curlew Dunes Memory Gardens. There will be food and drink afterward at our humble home for anyone who wishes to stay and share a memory of him. 

Please travel safely, 

Belinda Potts.
