Dear Corben,

I wish I were writing to you under happier circumstances.

My husband, [Valdemar Potts](Valdemar%20Potts.md), passed away from the sickness three days ago. We knew he was ill, and we knew what that could mean, but I don't think either of us was prepared for how quickly the end would come. In his final days, Valdemar spoke often about his life and the people who had been part of it. He spoke of you. Whatever roads may have taken you apart since you last saw one another, I want you to know that he remembered you fondly.

We will be laying Valdemar to rest here in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md). I know travel has become difficult, and perhaps even dangerous, so I understand if you cannot come. But if circumstances allow it, I would be grateful to have you here.

I think it would have meant the world to Valdemar to know that the people who mattered to him were together one last time on his behalf.

The funeral will be held on [Sulday the 7th of Vigus](..\Calendar,%20Time,%20&%20Holidays.md) at Curlew Dunes Memory Gardens. There will be food and drink afterward at our humble home for anyone who wishes to stay and share a memory of him.

Please travel safely,

Belinda Potts.
