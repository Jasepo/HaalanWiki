**Overview:** An elusive scholar or authenticator residing in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md). The recipient named on the dying royal messenger’s parcel, tasked by King Lambert with discreetly verifying an ancient relic before the next full moon.

* **Connections:** Central to the main mystery; potential expert on the Thousand Year War, the [Six Gods](..\Gods,%20Faith,%20&%20The%20Forsaken.md), or ancient ciphers.

 > 
 > \[!question\] The "Multiple Veyras" Revelation
 > The name "Mistress Veyra" is shared by multiple individuals in the region:
 > 
 > * **Marcus Veyra:** A shipping merchant at the Luvel docks. *(Lead provided by [Professor Marchenko](Professor%20Marchenko.md)).*
 > * **Mara Veyra:** Owner of Antiques and Curiosities. *(Lead provided by [Nixie](..\Session%20Summaries\2026-11-13.md) at [The Gilded Rose](..\Locations\Luvel.md)).*
 > * **Mirabel Veyra:** A retired Grand Arcanum scholar. (Lead provided by [Nixie](..\Session%20Summaries\2026-11-13.md) at [The Gilded Rose](..\Locations\Luvel.md)).\*
 > 
 > It is currently unknown which of these candidates is the true intended recipient of the royal parcel, or if they are all connected to the mystery.
