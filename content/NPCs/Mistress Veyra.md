**Overview:** An elusive scholar or authenticator residing in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md). The recipient named on the dying royal messenger’s parcel, tasked by King Lambert with discreetly verifying an ancient relic before the next full moon.

* **Connections:** Central to the main mystery; potential expert on the Thousand Year War, the [Six Gods](..\Gods,%20Faith,%20&%20The%20Forsaken.md), or ancient ciphers.
