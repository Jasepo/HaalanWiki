**Overview:** An elusive scholar or authenticator residing in [[Locations/Luvel|Luvel]]. The recipient named on the dying royal messenger’s parcel, tasked by King Lambert with discreetly verifying an ancient relic before the next full moon.

- **Connections:** Central to the main mystery; potential expert on the Thousand Year War, the [[Gods, Faith, & The Forsaken|Six Gods]], or ancient ciphers.

> [!question] The "Multiple Veyras" Revelation
> The name "Mistress Veyra" is shared by multiple individuals in the region:
> - **Marcus Veyra:** A shipping merchant at the Luvel docks. *(Lead provided by [[Professor Marchenko]]).*
> - **Mara Veyra:** Owner of Antiques and Curiosities. *(Lead provided by [[Session Summaries/2026-11-13|Nixie]] at [[Locations/Luvel|The Gilded Rose]]).*
> - **Mirabel Veyra:** A retired Grand Arcanum scholar. (Lead provided by [[Session Summaries/2026-11-13|Nixie]] at [[Locations/Luvel|The Gilded Rose]]).*
> 
> It is currently unknown which of these candidates is the true intended recipient of the royal parcel, or if they are all connected to the mystery.
