**Overview:** An elusive scholar or authenticator residing in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md). The recipient named on the dying royal messenger’s parcel, tasked by King Lambert with discreetly verifying an ancient relic before the next full moon.

* **Connections:** Central to the main mystery; potential expert on the Thousand Year War, the [Six Gods](..\Gods,%20Faith,%20&%20The%20Forsaken.md), or ancient ciphers.

### The "Multiple Veyras" Revelation

When the party began investigating the name "Mistress Veyra," they quickly discovered that the name is shared by multiple individuals in the region. It is currently unknown which of these candidates is the true intended recipient of the royal parcel, or if they are all connected to the mystery.

* **Marcus Veyra:** A shipping merchant operating out of the [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md) docks. *(Lead provided by [Professor Marchenko](Professor%20Marchenko.md)).*
* **Mara Veyra:** The owner of the shop [Antiques and Curiosities](..\Locations\Luvel.md) located in the [Crossways](..\Locations\Luvel.md). *(Lead provided by [Nixie](..\Session%20Summaries\2026-11-13.md) at [The Gilded Rose](..\Locations\Luvel.md)).*
* **Mirabel Veyra:** A retired scholar who previously studied at the [Grand Arcanum](..\Grand%20Arcanum.md) in [Borgan](..\Locations\Borgan.md). *(Lead provided by [Nixie](..\Session%20Summaries\2026-11-13.md) at [The Gilded Rose](..\Locations\Luvel.md)).*
