Retired halfling adventurer who now runs a merchant shop in Luvel alongside his wife, ***[Belinda Potts](Belinda%20Potts.md)***. On his adventuring days Valdemar spent years traveling all across Haalan, collecting stories, oddities, and strange items from his journeys. During his travels, Valdemar met a dwarven woman named Belinda. He fell deeply in love with her. Eventually, Belinda gave him an ultimatum: he could either settle down and stay with her, or continue his wandering adventuring ways without her. Realizing how much he loved her, Valdemar chose to hang up his adventuring boots for good. Valdemar opened up a merchant shop in Luvel. It functions almost like an oddities shop, filled with the vast collection of items and gear he accumulated over his years of adventuring. Even after all these years, he still hasn't managed to sell everything, meaning visitors can often find interesting and unique items there.
