Retired halfling adventurer who once ran a merchant shop in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md) alongside his wife, [Belinda Potts](Belinda%20Potts.md). During his adventuring days, Valdemar spent years traveling all across [Haalan](..\Locations\Haalan.md), collecting stories, oddities, and strange items from his journeys. In the course of his travels, Valdemar met a dwarven woman named [Belinda](Belinda%20Potts.md) and fell deeply in love with her. Eventually, [Belinda](Belinda%20Potts.md) gave him an ultimatum: he could either settle down and stay with her, or [continue](..\Locations\Haalan.md) his wandering adventuring ways without her. Realizing how much he loved her, Valdemar chose to hang up his adventuring boots for good and opened a merchant shop in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md). It functioned almost like an oddities shop, filled with the vast collection of items and gear he accumulated over his years of adventuring. Even after all these years, much of his collection remains unsold, meaning visitors can still find interesting and unique items there today.
