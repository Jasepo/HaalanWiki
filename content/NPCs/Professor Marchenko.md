A retired artificer and Firbolg teacher who resides in [[Luvel]] and overlooks the city's [[The Brasswright Guild|guild]] chapter. He has a workshop, private library, and hostel accommodations. He is one of the few allies in the [[The Brasswright Guild|guild]] who believes in [[Corben Wigglehand|Corben]]'s innocence and is working to expand the [[The Brasswright Guild|guild]]'s reach in [[Luvel]].
