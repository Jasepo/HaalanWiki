A retired artificer and Firbolg teacher who resides in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md) and overlooks the city's [guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md) chapter. He has a workshop, private library, and hostel accommodations. He is one of the few allies in the [guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md) who believes in [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md)'s innocence and is working to expand the [guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md)'s reach in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md).
