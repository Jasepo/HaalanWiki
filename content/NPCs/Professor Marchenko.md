**Overview:** Professor Marchenko is a retired artificer and teacher who oversees the [[The Brasswright Guild|Brasswright Guild]] chapter in [[Luvel]]. He is an elderly, 8-foot-2, graying Firbolg with oversized spectacles who can usually be found wearing a stained leather apron bristling with tools. He provides workshop space, a private library, and hostel accommodations to guild members.

- **The Automaton "Pip":** Marchenko is the creator of "Pip," a unique, animated six-legged brass automaton.
    
- **Guild Goals:** He is actively working to expand the guild's reach in [[Luvel]].


> [!note] Alliance with Corben
>He is one of the few allies in the guild who believes in [[Corben Wigglehand|Corben]]'s innocence. In fact, Marchenko strongly suspects that [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]]'s incident in [[Izedel]] was not an accident, but an orchestrated political sabotage against the Crown.
