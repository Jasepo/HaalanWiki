A retired artificer and Firbolg teacher who resides in Luvel and overlooks the city's [guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md) chapter. He has a workshop, private library, and hostel accommodations. He is one of the few allies in the guild who believes in [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md)'s innocence and is working to expand the guild's reach in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md).
