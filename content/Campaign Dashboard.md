[ ] **The Royal Courier’s Parcel**: A dying royal courier from [[Locations/Borgan|Borgan]] collapsed from [[Red Lung]] in the [[Locations/Luvel|Sunmarket]] and entrusted a sealed parcel to the party. He pleaded with his dying breath: "Don't let them take it... you must find [[Mistress Veyra]]." The package contains an ancient mechanical silver coin with six interlocking circles, a cryptic parchment ("The throne remembers what the crown has forgotten"), and a letter addressed to "[[Mistress Veyra]]" from the King, demanding discreet authentication before the next full moon while forbidding contact with the [[Grand Arcanum|Grand Arcanum]] or Royal Court. Royal Guards in crimson and gold are scouring the city for it.
