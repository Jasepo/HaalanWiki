# Active Quests

1. [ ] **The Royal Courier’s Parcel**: A dying royal courier from [Borgan](Locations\Borgan.md) collapsed from [Red Lung](Red%20Lung.md) in the [Sunmarket](Locations\Luvel.md) and entrusted a sealed parcel to the party. He pleaded with his dying breath: "Don't let them take it... you must find [Mistress Veyra](NPCs\Mistress%20Veyra.md)." The package contains an ancient mechanical silver coin with six interlocking circles, a cryptic parchment ("The throne remembers what the crown has forgotten"), and a letter addressed to "[Mistress Veyra](NPCs\Mistress%20Veyra.md)" from the King, demanding discreet authentication before the next full moon while forbidding contact with the [Grand Arcanum](Grand%20Arcanum.md) or Royal Court. Royal Guards in crimson and gold are scouring the city for it.

* **Current Leads on "Mistress Veyra":**
  
  * **Marcus Veyra:** A shipping merchant operating out of the [Luvel](Locations\Luvel.md) docks (Lead via [Professor Marchenko](NPCs\Professor%20Marchenko.md)).
  
  * **Mara Veyra:** Owner of the shop [Antiques and Curiosities](Locations\Luvel.md) in the [Crossways](Locations\Luvel.md) (Lead via Nixie).
  
  * **Mirabel Veyra:** A retired scholar who previously studied at the [Grand Arcanum](Grand%20Arcanum.md) in [Borgan](Locations\Borgan.md) (Lead via Nixie).

* **Current Leads on the silver coin:**
  
  * A book found in [Antiques and Curiosities](Locations\Luvel.md) documents the six-dial interlocking mechanical coin held by the party, confirming it represents the ancient [Gods](Gods,%20Faith,%20&%20The%20Forsaken.md)/[Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md) and noting that multiple such coins/keys exist across [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md).
