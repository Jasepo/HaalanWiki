* **Standard Ancestries:** Humans, dwarves, elves, halflings, and gnomes make up most of Haalani population. Orcs, goblins, and beastfolk are also established. Regional culture matters more than ancestry.

## Gnomes of Haalan:

* **Forest Gnomes:** From the region near [Mivolan](Locations\Mivolan.md).
* **Rock Gnomes:** From the region of [Izedel](Locations\Izedel.md).
* **Deep Gnomes:** From underground regions of [Mount Kazan](Locations\Mount%20Kazan.md) and [Umberdeep](The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md).
* *Traits:* Intelligent, cunning, highly industrious, capable of working on projects for days without stopping to eat or sleep.

## Dragonborn & Kobolds

* **Dragonborn:** Rare but welcome in modern Haalani society. A widespread legend claims that whenever a true dragon makes a wish, a Dragonborn comes to life, though Dragonborn themselves do not remember the circumstances of their creation.
* **Kobolds:** Rare and traditionally regard Dragonborn with a peculiar mixture of reverence, loyalty, imitation, and familial affection.

## Tieflings & Aasimar

* **Origins & Superstitions:** Either can be born to parents of any ancestry. They were historically interpreted as omens—Aasimar as favorable signs and Tieflings as troubling ones. These superstitions persist even in secular Haalan, though they say nothing about an individual's actual morality.

* **Other Ancestries:** Dragonborn & Kobolds, Tieflings & Aasimar, Goliaths (Hrothmar), Tabaxi (Marakai), Aarakocra (Caelora), and Fey/Satyr/Harengons (Everweald).
  
  ## Peoples from Beyond Haalan & Other Realms

* **Goliaths:** Originate from **Hrothmar**, the frozen northern homeland of giants.

* **Tabaxi:** Originate from **Marakai**, a vast tropical archipelago far to the east.

* **Aarakocra & Bird-folk:** Originate from **Caelora**, a dry and mountainous land to the southeast.

* **Fey (Fairies, Satyrs, Harengons):** Originate from the **Everweald**.
