
- **Standard Ancestries:** Humans, dwarves, elves, halflings, and gnomes make up most of [[Locations/Haalan|Haalani]] population. Orcs, goblins, and beastfolk are also established. Regional culture matters more than ancestry.

## Gnomes of Haalan:

- **Forest Gnomes:** From the region near [[Mivolan]].
- **Rock Gnomes:** From the region of [[Izedel]].
- **Deep Gnomes:** From [[The Umberdeep & Underground|underground]] regions of [[Mount Kazan]] and [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umberdeep]].
- _Traits:_ Intelligent, cunning, highly industrious, capable of working on projects for days without stopping to eat or sleep.

## Dragonborn & Kobolds

- **Dragonborn:** Rare but welcome in modern [[Locations/Haalan|Haalani]] society. A widespread legend claims that whenever a true dragon makes a wish, a Dragonborn comes to life, though Dragonborn themselves do not remember the circumstances of their creation.
- **Kobolds:** Rare and traditionally regard Dragonborn with a peculiar mixture of reverence, loyalty, imitation, and familial affection.


## Tieflings & Aasimar

- **Origins & Superstitions:** Either can be born to parents of any ancestry. They were historically interpreted as omens, Aasimar as favorable signs and Tieflings as troubling ones. These superstitions persist even in secular [[Locations/Haalan|Haalan]], though they say nothing about an individual's actual morality.
- **Other Ancestries:** Dragonborn & Kobolds, Tieflings & Aasimar, Goliaths (Hrothmar), Tabaxi (Marakai), Aarakocra (Caelora), and Fey/Satyr/Harengons ([[The Six Realms|Everweald]]).

  
## Peoples from Beyond Haalan & Other Realms

- **Goliaths:** Originate from **Hrothmar**, the frozen northern homeland of giants.

- **Tabaxi:** Originate from **Marakai**, a vast tropical archipelago far to the east.

- **Aarakocra & Bird-folk:** Originate from **Caelora**, a dry and mountainous land to the southeast.

- **Fey (Fairies, Satyrs, Harengons):** Originate from the **[[The Six Realms|Everweald]]**.

- **Harengons:** Rabbit-folk whose ancestors, people believe, originally migrated from the [[The Six Realms|Everweald]] long ago.
