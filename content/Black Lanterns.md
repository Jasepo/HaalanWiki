* **Overview:** A specialized faction headquartered in [Silverstone](Locations\Silverstone.md). Their primary operations take place within the [mountain ranges](Locations\Mount%20Kazan.md), focusing on combat and holding back monsters emerging from the [Umberdeep](The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md).

* **Public Perception:** Widely viewed by outsiders as a dark, shady, and mysterious organization.

* **Internal Culture & Code:** Operates as a tight-knit brotherhood rather than a government militia that treats their personnel as disposable cannon fodder. Members from the Black Lanterns are actively looked after and supported from within.

* **Traditions & Protocols:** Employs a strict code regarding rescue missions. When members fall in the field, the brotherhood prioritizes recovering them, even if only for their remains, to ensure a proper burial.

* **Insignia:** Members wear a distinctive black ring.

* **Notable Members:** [Thresher](PCs\Thresher.md) (former member).
