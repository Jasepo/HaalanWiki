**Overview:** A specialized faction headquartered in [[Silverstone]]. Widely viewed by outsiders as a dark, shady, and mysterious organization, their primary operations take place within the [[Locations/Mount Kazan|mountain ranges]], focusing on combat and holding back monsters emerging from the [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umberdeep]]. Members wear a distinctive black lantern tattoo.

- **Notable Members:** [[Thresher]] (former member).
