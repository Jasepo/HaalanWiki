**Overview:** A specialized faction headquartered in [Silverstone](Locations\Silverstone.md). Widely viewed by outsiders as a dark, shady, and mysterious organization, their primary operations take place within the [mountain ranges](Locations\Mount%20Kazan.md), focusing on combat and holding back monsters emerging from the [Umberdeep](The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md). Members wear a distinctive black lantern tattoo.

* **Notable Members:** [Thresher](PCs\Thresher.md) (former member).
