**Summary:**  
The campaign opened under the sweltering desert sun of [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md) at the [Curlew Dunes Memory Gardens](..\Locations\Luvel.md), where mourners gathered to lay retired adventurer and shopkeeper [Valdemar Potts](..\NPCs\Valdemar%20Potts.md) to rest. Officiated by the venerable orc priest [Yar Yuzul](..\NPCs\Yar%20Yuzul.md), the funeral brought together an unlikely trio of acquaintances: [Tripp Lockstep](..\PCs\Tripp%20Lockstep.md), who delivered a deadpan eulogy confessing that [Belinda Potts](..\NPCs\Belinda%20Potts.md) cheated at cards; [Corben Wigglehand](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md), who gifted a meticulously crafted reading display case containing the transcribed *Legend of the Three Fish*; and [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md), who slipped into the back row smelling of smoke and quietly buried two copper coins in the fresh soil to repay an old life debt. As mourners noticed the cemetery rapidly expanding to accommodate the rising toll of the [Red Lung](..\Red%20Lung.md), the gathering relocated to the Potts home in [The Crossways](..\Locations\Luvel.md) for food, drink, and remembrance.

At the wake, the party mingled with an eclectic crowd of mourners, while [Corben Wigglehand](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) obsessively cleaned surfaces (and guests) with Prestidigitation and bonded over caffeine addictions. To relieve the exhausted widow, [Belinda Potts](..\NPCs\Belinda%20Potts.md) dispatched the party to the bustling [Sunmarket](..\Locations\Luvel.md) on an errand run to collect ingredients for [Valdemar](..\NPCs\Valdemar%20Potts.md)'s memorial tart and lavender dumplings. Dividing and conquering across the vibrant market, the party navigated local commerce: securing flour from the jovial baker Fenwick Thistledown, herbs and nightshades from Madame Saffira, fresh produce and hygiene advice from Nessa Bramblewick, triple-shot espresso from Garrick Blackbrew, and goat milk from the eccentric herder Dorrin, the latter culminating in a frantic, coordinated tackle to capture an escaping prize goat named Eddie.

Their return trip was abruptly interrupted by an amateur children's street production of The Song of Six Crowns, recounting [Haalan](..\Locations\Haalan.md)'s bloody history, the retreat of the [gods](..\Gods,%20Faith,%20&%20The%20Forsaken.md), and the fragile peace under the newly crowned boy-king. Moments after the performance, commotion erupted as a traveler bearing the royal crest of [Borgan](..\Locations\Borgan.md) collapsed at their feet, bleeding and in the terminal throes of the [Red Lung](..\Red%20Lung.md). With his dying breath, the courier thrust a parcel toward them, pleading that they keep it from "them" and deliver it to someone named [Mistress Veyra](..\NPCs\Mistress%20Veyra.md). Snatching the parcel via Mage Hand just as foreign royal guards in crimson and gold arrived, the party slipped away back to [Valdemar](..\NPCs\Valdemar%20Potts.md)'s private study. Carefully loosening the wax seal, they uncovered an ancient silver coin with six mechanical, interlocking rotating dials, a cryptic Elvish note reading "The throne remembers what the crown has forgotten," and a secret royal mandate from the King himself, thrusting the reluctant party squarely into high-stakes treason.

**Key Events:**

* **The Burial of Valdemar Potts:** The party attended the funeral service led by [Yar Yuzul](..\NPCs\Yar%20Yuzul.md) at [Curlew Dunes Memory Gardens](..\Locations\Luvel.md), noting freshly dug graves expanding across the sands due to the [Red Lung](..\Red%20Lung.md).

* **The Wake at the Potts Residence:** Mourners gathered in [The Crossways](..\Locations\Luvel.md); [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) hid 5 gold pieces in [Valdemar](..\NPCs\Valdemar%20Potts.md)’s travel journal, [Corben Wigglehand](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) downed potent [Umberdeep](..\The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md) mushroom tea while sanitizing dishes, and [Tripp Lockstep](..\PCs\Tripp%20Lockstep.md) volunteered [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md)'s tinkering services to the [House of Open Pages](..\Locations\Luvel.md).

* **[Sunmarket](..\Locations\Luvel.md) Provisioning Run:** The party split up across the market stalls, procuring ingredients and wrangling a runaway prize goat at Butterbarrel's Best, earning an artisan cheese platter for their efforts.

* **The Song of Six Crowns:** The party witnessed a children's theatrical pageant illustrating [Haalan](..\Locations\Haalan.md)'s unification, the abandonment by the [Six Gods](..\Gods,%20Faith,%20&%20The%20Forsaken.md), and rising dread surrounding the succession of 15-year-old King Lambert.

* **The Dying Courier's Plea:** A royal messenger infected with the [Red Lung](..\Red%20Lung.md) collapsed in the market, begged the party to find [Mistress Veyra](..\NPCs\Mistress%20Veyra.md), and died, prompting a stealthy getaway as crimson-and-gold royal guards swarmed the scene.

* **Breaching the Royal Parcel:** In [Valdemar](..\NPCs\Valdemar%20Potts.md)'s upstairs study, [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) and [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) delicately bypassed the royal seal to reveal an ancient mechanical silver coin, an Elvish riddle, and a covert letter from the Crown bypassing the [Grand Arcanum](..\Grand%20Arcanum.md) and the Royal Court.
