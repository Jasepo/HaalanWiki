

- **Date:** Sulday, the 7th of Vigus, 275 A.W. – Varday, the 8th of Vigus, 275 A.W. (Session conducted: Session 02)
    
- **Location:** [[Luvel|Potts Residence]], [[Luvel|House of the Open Pages]], [[The Brasswright Guild]], [[Luvel|The Gilded Rose]], [[Luvel]], [[Luvel|Antiques and Curiosities]]
    
- **Party:** [[Corben Wigglehand]] (Jacob), [[Tripp Lockstep|Tripp]] (Joe), [[Thresher]] (Moose)
    

**Summary:**  
After resealing the fallen royal courier's mysterious package with melted wax, the party rejoins the wake at the [[Luvel|Potts Residence]] to hear humorous tales of the late [[Valdemar Potts]]. Following a sudden knock at the door that sends an overly cautious [[Thresher]] vaulting over back fences (only for the visitor to be Fenwick the baker bearing blueberry muffins), the companions make their way to the [[Luvel|House of the Open Pages]]. There, they perform charity handiwork, secure lodging from Elsbeth Wren, and [[Corben Wigglehand|Corben]] bonds with a young student by repairing a mechanical beetle using scrap metal tied to [[The Brasswright Guild]]. Along the way, [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]] confesses his dark secret: he is an exiled fugitive from [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]], framed after his prototype mechanical horse mysteriously exploded near a [[Locations/Borgan|Borgan]] diplomat.

Seeking sanctuary and information on the elusive "[[Mistress Veyra]]", the party visits [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]]’s old acquaintance [[Professor Marchenko]] at [[The Brasswright Guild]]. The towering Firbolg inventor agrees to shelter [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]] and pays the party 20 gold pieces each to recover his runaway automaton, Pip, from the workshop cellar. Venturing into the dark depths, the party stumbles upon a terrifying, bottomless chasm descending into the [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umber Deep]] and fends off a pair of aggressive giant rats in their first combat encounter. After [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]] pacifies and greases up the [[Rules & Mechanics|mechanical]] construct, they return Pip safely to [[NPCs/Professor Marchenko|Marchenko]], who points them toward a port merchant named Marcus Veyra.

Hoping for broader underworld leads, [[PCs/Thresher|Thresher]] guides the party to [[Luvel|The Gilded Rose]], a lavish brothel in the [[Locations/Luvel|Crossways]]. Consulting a goblin courtesan named Nixie, they learn of two other candidates: Mara Veyra, proprietor of an antique shop, and Mirabel Veyra, a retired scholar tied to the [[Grand Arcanum]]. After [[PCs/Thresher|Thresher]] visits his fence [[Caldeer]] to trade stolen wine for a grappling hook and receives a cryptic note about a woman hunting for him, the group stages a midnight break-in at [[Luvel|Antiques and Curiosities]]. Scaling up to a shattered upper window, they ransack the office and discover a tome detailing ancient [[Gods, Faith, & The Forsaken|gods]] and depicting the exact seal of the coin-key in their possession, only to freeze as a familiar Tabaxi smuggler named Rys chuckles from the shadows above.

**Key Events:**

- The party successfully reseals the royal courier’s wax letter, leaving it concealed in [[Thresher]]'s coat, and [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]] pockets five pinches of [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]]’s sweet cherry tobacco.
    
- The companions assist at the [[Luvel|House of the Open Pages]]: [[PCs/Tripp Lockstep|Tripp]] fixes sagging bookshelves, [[PCs/Thresher|Thresher]] boils flour paste in the kitchen, and [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]] repairs a child's mechanical beetle.
    
- [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]] reveals his backstory to the party: he fled [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]] after his non-explosive mechanical horse detonated during an inventor's fair, suspecting political sabotage by rivals seeking to assassinate a [[Locations/Borgan|Borgan]] diplomat.
    
- At [[The Brasswright Guild]], [[Professor Marchenko]] hires the group for 20 gp each to retrieve his lost automaton, Pip, from the basement.
    
- **Combat Encounter:** In the dark basement, the party encounters giant rats near a fathomless hole leading straight to the [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umberdeep]]. [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]] suffers a vicious bite to the neck, [[PCs/Tripp Lockstep|Tripp]] casts Bless, and [[PCs/Thresher|Thresher]] eliminates the vermin with a concealed hand crossbow. [[PCs/Corben Wigglehand|Corben]] heals himself and coaxes Pip onto his shoulder.
    
- [[PCs/Tripp Lockstep|Tripp]] drops a light-infused potato into the basement chasm, confirming it drops endlessly into the [[The Umberdeep & Underground]]; they warn [[NPCs/Professor Marchenko|Marchenko]] to seal it.
    
- At [[Luvel|The Gilded Rose]], Nixie provides two vital leads on the [[NPCs/Mistress Veyra|Veyra]] name: Mara Veyra (antique shop owner in the [[Locations/Luvel|Crossways]]) and Mirabel Veyra (retired scholar of the [[Grand Arcanum]]).
    
- [[PCs/Thresher|Thresher]] visits [[Caldeer]] at [[Luvel|The Broken Shillelagh]], fences stolen basement wine, purchases a grappling hook, and receives a note indicating a strange woman is searching for him.
    
- Past midnight on the 8th of [[Calendar, Time, & Holidays|Vigus]], the party infiltrates [[Luvel|Antiques and Curiosities]] through a second-story window; [[PCs/Tripp Lockstep|Tripp]] loots a glowing magic candle.
    
- In the [[Locations/Luvel|antique]] shop office, the party matches their mysterious coin-key to an illustration in a historical text on ancient deities before being interrupted by Rys, a notorious Tabaxi smuggler.
