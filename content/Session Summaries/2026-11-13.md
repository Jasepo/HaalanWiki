* **Location:** [Potts Residence](..\Locations\Luvel.md), [House of the Open Pages](..\Locations\Luvel.md), [The Brasswright Guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md), [The Gilded Rose](..\Locations\Luvel.md), [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md), [Antiques and Curiosities](..\Locations\Luvel.md)

* **Party:** [Corben Wigglehand](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) (Jacob), [Tripp](..\PCs\Tripp%20Lockstep.md) (Joe), [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) (Moose)

**Summary:**  
After resealing the fallen royal courier's mysterious package with melted wax, the party rejoins the wake at the [Potts Residence](..\Locations\Luvel.md) to hear humorous tales of the late [Valdemar Potts](..\NPCs\Valdemar%20Potts.md). Following a sudden knock at the door that sends an overly cautious [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) vaulting over back fences (only for the visitor to be Fenwick the baker bearing blueberry muffins), the companions make their way to the [House of the Open Pages](..\Locations\Luvel.md). There, they perform charity handiwork, secure lodging from Elsbeth Wren, and [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) bonds with a young student by repairing a mechanical beetle using scrap metal tied to [The Brasswright Guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md). Along the way, [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) confesses his dark secret: he is an exiled fugitive from [Izedel](..\Locations\Izedel.md), framed after his prototype mechanical horse mysteriously exploded near a [Borgan](..\Locations\Borgan.md) diplomat.

Seeking sanctuary and information on the elusive "[Mistress Veyra](..\NPCs\Mistress%20Veyra.md)", the party visits [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md)’s old acquaintance [Professor Marchenko](..\NPCs\Professor%20Marchenko.md) at [The Brasswright Guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md). The towering Firbolg inventor agrees to shelter [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) and pays the party 20 gold pieces each to recover his runaway automaton, Pip, from the workshop cellar. Venturing into the dark depths, the party stumbles upon a terrifying, bottomless chasm descending into the [Umber Deep](..\The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md) and fends off a pair of aggressive giant rats in their first combat encounter. After [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) pacifies and greases up the [mechanical](..\Rules%20&%20Mechanics.md) construct, they return Pip safely to [Marchenko](..\NPCs\Professor%20Marchenko.md), who points them toward a port merchant named Marcus Veyra.

Hoping for broader underworld leads, [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) guides the party to [The Gilded Rose](..\Locations\Luvel.md), a lavish brothel in the [Crossways](..\Locations\Luvel.md). Consulting a goblin courtesan named Nixie, they learn of two other candidates: Mara Veyra, proprietor of an antique shop, and Mirabel Veyra, a retired scholar tied to the [Grand Arcanum](..\Grand%20Arcanum.md). After [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) visits his fence [Caldeer](..\NPCs\Caldeer.md) to trade stolen wine for a grappling hook and receives a cryptic note about a woman hunting for him, the group stages a midnight break-in at [Antiques and Curiosities](..\Locations\Luvel.md). Scaling up to a shattered upper window, they ransack the office and discover a tome detailing ancient [gods](..\Gods,%20Faith,%20&%20The%20Forsaken.md) and depicting the exact seal of the coin-key in their possession, only to freeze as a familiar Tabaxi smuggler named Rys chuckles from the shadows above.

**Key Events:**

* The party successfully reseals the royal courier’s wax letter, leaving it concealed in [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md)'s coat, and [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) pockets five pinches of [Valdemar](..\NPCs\Valdemar%20Potts.md)’s sweet cherry tobacco.

* The companions assist at the [House of the Open Pages](..\Locations\Luvel.md): [Tripp](..\PCs\Tripp%20Lockstep.md) fixes sagging bookshelves, [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) boils flour paste in the kitchen, and [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) repairs a child's mechanical beetle.

* [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) reveals his backstory to the party: he fled [Izedel](..\Locations\Izedel.md) after his non-explosive mechanical horse detonated during an inventor's fair, suspecting political sabotage by rivals seeking to assassinate a [Borgan](..\Locations\Borgan.md) diplomat.

* At [The Brasswright Guild](..\The%20Brasswright%20Guild.md), [Professor Marchenko](..\NPCs\Professor%20Marchenko.md) hires the group for 20 gp each to retrieve his lost automaton, Pip, from the basement.

* **Combat Encounter:** In the dark basement, the party encounters giant rats near a fathomless hole leading straight to the [Umberdeep](..\The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md). [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) suffers a vicious bite to the neck, [Tripp](..\PCs\Tripp%20Lockstep.md) casts Bless, and [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) eliminates the vermin with a concealed hand crossbow. [Corben](..\PCs\Corben%20Wigglehand.md) heals himself and coaxes Pip onto his shoulder.

* [Tripp](..\PCs\Tripp%20Lockstep.md) drops a light-infused potato into the basement chasm, confirming it drops endlessly into the [The Umberdeep & Underground](..\The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md); they warn [Marchenko](..\NPCs\Professor%20Marchenko.md) to seal it.

* At [The Gilded Rose](..\Locations\Luvel.md), Nixie provides two vital leads on the [Veyra](..\NPCs\Mistress%20Veyra.md) name: Mara Veyra (antique shop owner in the [Crossways](..\Locations\Luvel.md)) and Mirabel Veyra (retired scholar of the [Grand Arcanum](..\Grand%20Arcanum.md)).

* [Thresher](..\PCs\Thresher.md) visits [Caldeer](..\NPCs\Caldeer.md) at [The Broken Shillelagh](..\Locations\Luvel.md), fences stolen basement wine, purchases a grappling hook, and receives a note indicating a strange woman is searching for him.

* Past midnight on the 8th of [Vigus](..\Calendar,%20Time,%20&%20Holidays.md), the party infiltrates [Antiques and Curiosities](..\Locations\Luvel.md) through a second-story window; [Tripp](..\PCs\Tripp%20Lockstep.md) loots a glowing magic candle.

* In the [antique](..\Locations\Luvel.md) shop office, the party matches their mysterious coin-key to an illustration in a historical text on ancient deities before being interrupted by Rys, a notorious Tabaxi smuggler.

* [Caldeer](..\NPCs\Caldeer.md): A pragmatic fence operating out of [The Broken Shillelagh](..\Locations\Luvel.md) who buys stolen goods.
