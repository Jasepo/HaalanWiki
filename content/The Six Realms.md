1. **The Mortal World:** Physical surface world of [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md) and surrounding areas.

1. **The Everweald:** The fey realm of ancient magic, strange beauty, untamed nature, madness, and lack of balance.

1. **The Umberdeep:** A vast subterranean physical world beneath the surface of [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md).

1. **The Infernal Reach:** Realm of fiends and demons.

1. **The First Breath:** Primordial realm of elemental forces.

1. **The Final Shore:** Realm of the dead and the passage of mortal souls.

#### Realm Nature & Crossover

* **Physical vs. In-Between:** The Mortal World and the *Umberdeep* are the only two real physical realms, directly connected by depth beneath the surface. All other realms are metaphysical.
* **The Final Shore:** The plane of the dead. Traditional burial practices serve as a send-off to the Final Shore, which some believe is located between the surface soil and the [Umberdeep](The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md).
* **The Everwild:** Equivalent to the fey realm. Travel is dangerous and life-altering, though thrill-seekers or substance users occasionally access it. It serves as the ancestral origin for species such as *Harengons*.
* **First Breath & Infernal Reach:** Crossover from the First Breath to the mortal world is extremely rare.
