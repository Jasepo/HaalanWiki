1. **The Mortal World:** Physical surface world of [[Haalan]] and surrounding areas.

2. **The Everweald:** The fey realm of ancient magic, strange beauty, untamed nature, madness, and lack of balance.

3. **The Umberdeep:** A vast subterranean physical world beneath the surface of [[Haalan]].

4. **The Infernal Reach:** Realm of fiends and demons.

5. **The First Breath:** Primordial realm of elemental forces.

6. **The Final Shore:** Realm of the dead and the passage of mortal souls.

#### Realm Nature & Crossover

- **Physical vs. In-Between:** The Mortal World and the [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umberdeep]] are the only two real physical realms, directly connected by depth beneath the surface. All other realms are metaphysical.
- **The Final Shore:** The plane of the dead. Traditional burial practices serve as a send-off to the Final Shore, which some believe is located between the surface soil and the [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umberdeep]].
- **The Everweald:** Equivalent to the fey realm. Travel is dangerous and life-altering, though thrill-seekers or substance users occasionally access it. It serves as the ancestral origin for species such as [[Character Ancestries & Origins|Harengons]].
- **First Breath & Infernal Reach:** Crossover from the First Breath to the mortal world is extremely rare.
