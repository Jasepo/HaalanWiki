**Overview:** The official tinkers' guild of [[Locations/Haalan|Haalan]], representing inventors, tinkers, engineers, clockmakers, and artificers throughout the nation. Its most prominent chapters are located in [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]], [[Locations/Borgan|Borgan]], and [[Locations/Silverstone|Silverstone]].

> [!info] Benefits & Features
> - Members receive lodging, food, and funeral benefits if needed.
> - Guildhalls offer a central place to meet patrons, allies, hirelings, and access tools, supplies, and resources.
> - Guilds wield political power and will support members accused of a crime if innocence can be argued.
> - Dues are 5 gp per month (already paid for the month of [[Calendar, Time, & Holidays|Vigus]]).

### Key Figures
- **[[Professor Marchenko]]**: The Firbolg overseer of the [[Locations/Luvel|Luvel]] chapter. He can usually be found on the third floor in Workshop 17.

## The Luvel Guildhall

Located in the [[Locations/Luvel|Crossways]] of [[Locations/Luvel|Luvel]], the local Guildhall is a sprawling three-story workshop of sandstone, dark timber, and reinforced iron. It is easily recognizable by its three brass exterior signs (a crossed hammer and drafting compass), a backwards-moving clock on the wall, and suspended catwalks. 

Entering the building requires navigating a rigid bureaucratic intake (Forms A through E) managed by an exhausted [[Character Ancestries & Origins|gnome]] receptionist. 

> [!danger] The Bottomless Pit
> A massive, unfathomably deep vertical chasm in the guild's cellar descends directly into the [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umberdeep]]. The party warned [[Professor Marchenko]] of the breach, only to discover he was completely unaware of its existence.
