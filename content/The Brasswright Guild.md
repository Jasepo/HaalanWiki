**Overview:** The official tinkers' guild of [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md), representing inventors, tinkers, engineers, clockmakers, and artificers throughout the nation. Its most prominent chapters are located in [Izedel](Locations\Izedel.md), [Borgan](Locations\Borgan.md), and [Silverstone](Locations\Silverstone.md).

 > 
 > \[!info\] Benefits & Features
 > 
 > * Members receive lodging, food, and funeral benefits if needed.
 > * Guildhalls offer a central place to meet patrons, allies, hirelings, and access tools, supplies, and resources.
 > * Guilds wield political power and will support members accused of a crime if innocence can be argued.
 > * Dues are 5 gp per month (already paid for the month of [Vigus](Calendar,%20Time,%20&%20Holidays.md)).

### Key Figures

* **[Professor Marchenko](NPCs\Professor%20Marchenko.md)**: The Firbolg overseer of the [Luvel](Locations\Luvel.md) chapter. He can usually be found on the third floor in Workshop 17.

## The Luvel Guildhall

Located in the [Crossways](Locations\Luvel.md) of [Luvel](Locations\Luvel.md), the local Guildhall is a sprawling three-story workshop of sandstone, dark timber, and reinforced iron. It is easily recognizable by its three brass exterior signs (a crossed hammer and drafting compass), a backwards-moving clock on the wall, and suspended catwalks.

Entering the building requires navigating a rigid bureaucratic intake (Forms A through E) managed by an exhausted [gnome](Character%20Ancestries%20&%20Origins.md) receptionist.

 > 
 > \[!danger\] The Bottomless Pit
 > A massive, unfathomably deep vertical chasm in the guild's cellar descends directly into the [Umberdeep](The%20Umberdeep%20&%20Underground.md). The party warned [Professor Marchenko](NPCs\Professor%20Marchenko.md) of the breach, only to discover he was completely unaware of its existence and the giant rats it attracted.
