- **Overview:** The official name of [[Locations/Haalan|Haalan]]'s tinkers' guild representing inventors, tinkers, engineers, clockmakers, and artificers throughout [[Locations/Haalan|Haalan]]. Its most prominent chapters are located in [[Izedel]], [[Borgan]], and [[Black Lanterns|Silverstone]].

- **Benefits & Features:**

    - Members receive lodging, food, and funeral pay if needed.
    
    - Guildhalls offer a central place to meet patrons, allies, hirelings, and access tools, supplies, and resources.
    
    - Guilds wield political power and will support members accused of a crime if innocence can be argued.
    
    - Dues are 5 gp per month (already paid for the month of [[Calendar, Time, & Holidays|Vigus]]).
    
- **Key NPC:** **[[Professor Marchenko]]**
