* **Overview:** The official name of [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md)'s tinkers' guild representing inventors, tinkers, engineers, clockmakers, and artificers throughout [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md). Its most prominent chapters are located in [Izedel](Locations\Izedel.md), [Borgan](Locations\Borgan.md), and [Silverstone](Black%20Lanterns.md).

* **Benefits & Features:**
  
  * Members receive lodging, food, and funeral benefits if needed.
  
  * Guildhalls offer a central place to meet patrons, allies, hirelings, and access tools, supplies, and resources.
  
  * Guilds wield political power and will support members accused of a crime if innocence can be argued.
  
  * Dues are 5 gp per month (already paid for the month of [Vigus](Calendar,%20Time,%20&%20Holidays.md)).

* **Key NPC:** **[Professor Marchenko](NPCs\Professor%20Marchenko.md)**
