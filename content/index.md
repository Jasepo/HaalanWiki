## The Six Regions

The six crowns that became one:

* **[Borgan](Locations/Borgan.md)** — seat of the Crown, political and magical heart of the nation
* **[Drabasha](Locations/Drabasha.md)** — swampy western loyalists, tied to Borgan by need
* **[Izedel](Locations/Izedel.md)** — cosmopolitan eastern port, home of the three fish
* **[Luvel](Locations/Luvel.md)** — neutral commercial hub controlling the central waterway *(starting location)*
* **[Mivolan](Locations/Mivolan.md)** — northern forest power, watching Borgan closely
* **[Regalla](Locations/Regalla.md)** — maritime northern islands questioning their place in the union
