## The Six Regions

The six crowns that became one:

- **[[Borgan]]** — seat of the Crown, political and magical heart of the nation
- **[[Drabasha]]** — swampy western loyalists, tied to Borgan by need
- **[[Izedel]]** — cosmopolitan eastern port, home of the three fish
- **[[Luvel]]** — neutral commercial hub controlling the central waterway *(starting location)*
- **[[Mivolan]]** — northern forest power, watching Borgan closely
- **[[Regalla]]** — maritime northern islands questioning their place in the union
