**Overview:** A seasoned rogue and former member of the **[Black Lanterns](..\Black%20Lanterns.md)** stationed in [Luvel](..\Locations\Luvel.md). He resides in the upstairs quarters of the *Broken Shillelagh* bar in the [Shallows district](..\Locations\Luvel.md), operated by [Caldeer](..\NPCs\Caldeer.md). He still wears the distinctive black ring of his former [brotherhood](..\Black%20Lanterns.md).
