**Overview:** A seasoned rogue and former member of the **[[Black Lanterns|Black Lanterns]]** stationed in [[Locations/Luvel|Luvel]]. He resides in the upstairs quarters of the _Broken Shillelagh_ bar in the [[Luvel|Shallows district]], operated by [[NPCs/Caldeer|Caldeer]]. He still wears the distinctive black ring of his former [[Black Lanterns|brotherhood]].
