Corben was a promising inventor in [[Izedel]]. At the grand Inventor’s Fair, he presented a four-legged mechanical horse. But his experimental arcane cores were sabotaged, causing a catastrophic explosion that killed a visiting [[Borgan]] diplomat. Falsely branded an assassin, Corben fled to the remote town of [[Izedel|Frostfall]] to hide with his uncle. When Crown spies tracked him there, the terrified [[Izedel|Frostfall]] town council banished Corben to protect the town from royal retribution. He left to keep them safe, planning to head toward the capital of [[Borgan]] to find the real killer and gather the rare materials he needs to build a new, safer mechanical mount.

## Goals:

- Short-term: Find the saboteur who tampered with his machine and clear his name.

- Long-term: Perfect the blueprints for his four-legged Steel Defender so he can ride it into battle, redeem his reputation, and return to [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]] as a respected Artificer.

## Connections:  
  

- Borin Cogspark (Uncle/Mentor): A retired Rock [[Character Ancestries & Origins|Gnome]] artificer living in [[Izedel|Frostfall]]. He believes Corben is innocent and secretly sends him letters with tinkering advice. He is too old to fight but provides emotional support.

- Unknown Saboteur (Rival/Suspect): An inventor from [[Borgan]] who took second place at the fair. This person was seen arguing with the diplomat just before the explosion. Corben is convinced they framed him.  
  
- Ortina Wigglehand (Mother): A respected jeweler in [[Izedel]]. She sent Corben a secret letter warning him not to come home, but she slipped him the schematics for the Steel Defender and some gold. She is currently under Crown surveillance.

## Flaw: 

Corben is a genius, but he is pathologically obsessed with cleanliness and order. He cannot stand grease on his hands. He constantly uses Prestidigitation to clean himself, his companions, his clothes, and his tools. He refuses to drink anything alcoholic, claiming regular ale "weakens the mind" and that water is enough. He talks to his tools and his Steel Defender as if they are living pets. If he sees a messy room or a poorly maintained machine, he must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw to resist the urge to stop everything and clean or fix it.

  
## Reasons to Cooperate in a Party:

Corben is a wanted man with no money and no safe haven. He knows he cannot clear his name, perfect his Steel Defender, or survive the Crown's assassins alone. He is fiercely loyal to anyone who offers him a safe place to tinker and believes in his innocence.

## **How I met your ~~mother~~ Valdemar Potts**

**The Fixer of [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]]**

[[Valdemar Potts]] had been wandering for three months. He had crossed the Everweald, skirted the edge of the [[The Umberdeep & Underground|Umberdeep]], and traded half his rations for a strange, broken trinket from a dying sailor in a port town. The sailor had called it a "Moon-lighter", a clockwork navigational device from the old days, said to be able to pinpoint the hidden temples of the Moon God.

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] didn't care about the Moon God. He cared about stories. And this little broken thing had a story written all over it.

But first, he needed to get it fixed.

He arrived in [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]] in the middle of a rainstorm. The city was massive, cosmopolitan, and loud. Three fish symbols were painted on every tavern sign and every ship sail. Travelers from a dozen nations jostled through the streets, speaking in a dozen languages. [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]], a halfling who had spent most of his life on the road, felt both overwhelmed and exhilarated.

He asked around for a tinkerer. A blacksmith. Someone who could fix a delicate piece of ancient clockwork.

Most people laughed at him. “You'd have better luck asking a wizard to do an honest day's work. Go find a jeweler, or stop wasting my time.”

But one old dockworker scratched his chin. "There's a gnome," he said. "Third floor of the old warehouse on Siren Street. Weird little fellow. Talks to his tools. Refuses to touch anything dirty. But they say he can fix anything."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] found the building. He climbed three flights of rickety wooden stairs, his halfling feet quiet on the steps. At the top, there was a door slightly ajar. He pushed it open.

The workshop was chaos. Gears and springs covered every surface. Blueprints were pinned to the walls. A half-finished mechanical leg sat on a workbench, its joints gleaming. And in the center of it all, a young rock gnome with grease on his sleeve and a Prestidigitation spell on his lips was muttering to a wrench.

"No, no, no," the gnome said. "You're stripping the threads. Do you have any idea how difficult it is to find a replacement gear of this size in [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]]? Of course you don't. YOU'RE A WRENCH!!!!."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] cleared his throat. "Are you Corben Wigglehand?"

The gnome spun around. His eyes were sharp, brilliant, and slightly unfocused. He looked at [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]], then at the mud on [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]]'s boots, then back at [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]]'s face.

"I am," Corben said. "And you are tracking dirt into my workshop."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] laughed. "I am [[Valdemar Potts]]. I'm an adventurer. I have a broken thing, and I heard you're the best fixer in [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]]."

Corben raised an eyebrow. "I am the only fixer in [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]] who will touch ancient clockwork. Show me the thing."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] reached into his pack and pulled out the Moon-lighter. It was caked in rust, salt, and something that smelled vaguely of seaweed. Corben recoiled as if [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] had handed him a live rat.

"Absolutely not," Corben said. "Clean it first."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] blinked. "It's a thousand-year-old artifact, my friend. The grime is part of the history."

"The grime is a biological hazard," Corben said. He grabbed the device from [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]]'s hands, cast Prestidigitation, and the rust and salt vanished. The Moon-lighter gleamed under the workshop's lantern light.

Corben held it up. His eyes softened. "Oh," he whispered. "Oh, this is beautiful."

He spent the next hour lost in the device. He didn't ask [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] any questions. He simply took it apart, analyzed every gear, and muttered about torque ratios and lunar alignments. [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] watched, fascinated. This gnome was clearly brilliant, but he was also completely unhinged. He talked to the gears as if they were old friends. He cleaned his hands three times during the process. And when he finally snapped the last piece back into place, the Moon-lighter hummed to life, its small needle quivering and pointing toward the window.

"There," Corben said. "It was a broken escapement. A child's mistake. It's fixed."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] reached for his coin purse. "How much do I owe you?"

Corben waved a hand. "Keep your gold. I don't want it."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] frowned. "Then what do you want?"

Corben looked at him. For the first time, his eyes focused completely on [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]]'s face. "You're an adventurer. You've traveled across [[Locations/Haalan|Haalan]]. I want a story. Tell me something I haven't read in a book."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] smiled. He sat down on a stool that was too small for him and thought for a moment. "Have you ever heard the legend of the Three Fish of [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]]?"

Corben leaned forward. "I'm from [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]]. Of course I have."

"Ah," [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] said. "But have you heard the real version? The one where the fish were not a symbol of unity, but a warning?"

Corben's eyes widened. He pulled up a stool of his own. "Go on."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] told him the story. It took an hour. By the end, Corben was furiously scribbling notes in the margins of his blueprint. When [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] finished, Corben looked up with a grin.

"That," Corben said, "was worth more than any gold."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] stood up and stretched. "I'll be back in [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]] eventually," he said. "I want to see what you build next."

Corben pointed to the half-finished mechanical leg on his workbench. "It's going to have four legs," he said. "And it's going to be magnificent."

[[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] laughed. "I don't doubt it for a second."

He left the workshop, closing the door gently behind him. But true to his word, [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] never disappeared from Corben's life. From wherever his travels took him across [[Locations/Haalan|Haalan]], he sent letters. Sometimes they were filled with grand tales of the Everweald. Other times, they were just short notes asking if Corben had finally finished his four-legged machine. He would occasionally include small trinkets he found on the road, a smooth stone from the Aerrton Sea, a bent gear from an old ruin. And every time [[NPCs/Valdemar Potts|Valdemar]] passed through [[Locations/Izedel|Izedel]], he would climb those three flights of rickety stairs, knock on the workshop door, and tell Corben more stories.
