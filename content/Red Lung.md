A natural, non-magical respiratory illness active in [Haalan](Locations\Haalan.md) for approximately 9 months since the start of the campaign [(Sulday, the 7th of Vigus, 275 A.W.)](Calendar,%20Time,%20&%20Holidays.md). It causes severe breathing strain, coughing blood, and death. Due to overwhelmed healing resources, fatalities are high, driving growth in the forbidden worship of [Velshara](Gods,%20Faith,%20&%20The%20Forsaken.md).

* **Impact & Care:** Though treatable with divine healing magic, the rapid spread of the disease overwhelms available clerics and priests, leading to high mortality rates in communities lacking access to medical care.

**Notable Victims:** [Valdemar Potts](NPCs\Valdemar%20Potts.md)
