
A natural, non-magical respiratory illness active in [[Locations/Haalan|Haalan]] for approximately 9 months since the start of the campaign [[Calendar, Time, & Holidays|(Sulday, the 7th of Vigus, 275 A.W.)]]. It causes severe breathing strain, coughing blood, and death. Due to overwhelmed healing resources, fatalities are high, driving growth in the forbidden worship of [[Gods, Faith, & The Forsaken|Velshara]].

- **Impact & Care:** Though treatable with divine healing magic, the rapid spread of the disease overwhelms available clerics and priests, leading to high mortality rates in communities lacking access to medical care.
